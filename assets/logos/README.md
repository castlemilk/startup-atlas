# Logo sources

These brand marks are self-hosted for identification; their inclusion does not imply a commercial relationship or eligibility for an offer. Unreviewed or ambiguous names deliberately fall back to initials. The names-to-logos mapping is maintained in `logos.js`.

- The following SVGs are from [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/tree/master/packages/static-svg/icons): `openai`, `aws-color`, `microsoft-color`, `xai`, `together`, `fireworks`, `baseten`, `deepinfra`, `nebius`, `replit`, `devin`, `cerebras`, `alibabacloud`, `github`, `googlecloud`, `anthropic`, `vercel`, `google-color` and `nvidia-color`. Lobe Icons is distributed under the MIT license; see `lobe-icons-MIT.txt`.
- `modal`, `producthunt`, `ycombinator`, `cloudflare`, `neon`, `mongodb`, `elevenlabs`, `deepgram`, `posthog`, `digitalocean`, `vultr`, `stripe`, `atlassian`, `notion`, `intercom` and `hubspot` are from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons). The project is distributed under CC0; see `simple-icons-CC0.txt`.
- `oracle` is from [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/blob/master/icons/oracle/oracle-original.svg); see `devicon-MIT.txt`.

Brand names and marks remain the property of their respective owners. Before adding a new logo, establish the identity and record its source and applicable terms.
