## Brand & Style

This design system serves an editorial startup ecosystem intelligence platform. It merges the gravitas of investigative business publications (e.g., Financial Times, The Economist) with the rigorous precision of institutional financial databases and modern ecosystem graph intelligence tools.

The interface evokes authoritative discernment, structural calm, and analytical clarity. It rejects noisy, hyper-saturated venture dashboards in favor of an archival, paper-grounded experience where long-form narrative commentary seamlessly frames quantitative evidence, relationship graphs, cap tables, and company comparisons.

The visual direction is grounded in an **Editorial Minimalist** movement: warm tactile paper surfaces, deeply saturated British racing/forest greens, stark white data panels, sharp fine borders, and a stark terracotta/rust accent used strictly for directional focus. Typography plays a central role: an expressive literary serif brings warmth and analytical depth to headlines and editorial dossiers, while a high-density, neutral grotesque handles multi-column tables, metadata tags, and dense network visualizations.

## Colors

The color palette establishes an archival reading environment anchored by tactile paper tones and deep forest inks:

- **Primary (`#1B382B` & `#122B22`)**: The structural spine. Used for primary navigation headers, dominant editorial headings, active states, key metric callouts, and grounding containers.
- **Secondary (`#C85A32` / Rust Accent)**: A focused, deliberate highlight color. Reserved strictly for primary action buttons, pinned items, high-priority interactive badges, and critical editorial callouts.
- **Neutrals (`#F9F8F5`, `#F4F1EA`, `#EFECE4`)**: Multi-tiered paper tones representing physical sheet layers. The page body floats on warm paper canvas (`#F4F1EA`), while cards and analytical tables leverage crisp optical white (`#FFFFFF`) to present uncompromised contrast against dense tabular data.
- **Entity Identification System**: Ecosystem participants must be identifiable at a glance without visual discord. Five dedicated pastel-tinted containers paired with high-contrast tinted ink provide distinct semantic entity typing:
  - **Startup**: Deep Teal (`#E9F1F0` / `#1F4E4B`)
  - **Person / Founder**: Warm Amber (`#FAF1E3` / `#875317`)
  - **Partner / VC**: Soft Indigo (`#EBEFF9` / `#2C468A`)
  - **Provider**: Muted Sage (`#EFF3ED` / `#38543B`)
  - **Programme / Accelerator**: Terracotta (`#F8EDE7` / `#91381E`)

## Typography

The typographical cadence balances long-form journalistic narrative with surgical data density:

- **Editorial Display & Headlines (`Newsreader`)**: Newsreader is applied to page headers, ecosystem briefs, entity titles, and thematic quotes. Optical sizing creates warm, literary authority without feeling antique. It supports italic styling for contextual subtitles and evidentiary citations.
- **Interface & Operational Typography (`Inter`)**: Inter drives all relational tables, filters, navigation bars, chip taxonomies, and entity profiles.
- **Numerical Alignment**: Tabular figures (`tnum`) must be enforced across all pricing tables, cap tables, valuation matrices, and date stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is structured on a strict 12-column responsive fluid grid designed for high-density analysis:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with 24px (`gutter-desktop`) gutters and 48px (`margin-desktop`) outer padding. Supports multi-pane inspector panels, side-by-side comparative matrices, and interactive treemaps. Max content boundary is capped at 1440px for comfortable reading lines.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8 columns with 16px gutters and 32px margins. Secondary inspector rails collapse into expandable drawer overlays.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with 16px gutters and 16px margins. Complex tabular grids collapse into discrete record cards with horizontal swipe affordances for metadata columns.

Spacing maintains a condensed rhythm: 4px and 8px primitives govern table padding and chip enclosures, while 20px (`space-xl`) and 48px (`space-2xl`) define macroscopic editorial chapters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is achieved through physical sheet stacking and precise hairlines rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Tonal Canvas Layers**: The physical depth is built in three primary tiers:
  - Base canvas (`#F4F1EA`): The underlying application foundation.
  - Recessed trays (`#EFECE4`): Filter toolbars, search headers, and relationship graph viewports.
  - Active record cards (`#FFFFFF`): Raised primary data layers.
- **Delicate Editorial Borders**: Crisp 1px hairlines (`#E2DDD3`) delineate panels, table rows, and split screens. Active or focused panels deepen to `#B5AEA2` or `#1B382B`.
- **Soft Ambient Shadows**: Floated cards, popovers, and context menus employ a single warm, diffused shadow tinted with forest ink: `0 2px 8px rgba(27, 56, 43, 0.04), 0 8px 24px rgba(27, 56, 43, 0.06)`. Modals utilize an additional semi-opaque parchment wash backdrop (`rgba(27, 56, 43, 0.25)`).

## Shapes

The design system maintains a structured, print-inspired geometry. Shape language reflects micro-softened editorial precision:

- Base elements (cards, input fields, dropdown menus, table records) utilize `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii (`roundedness: 1`), keeping elements crisp and document-like.
- Entity badges and operational chips scale to `0.25rem` for compact technical tag aesthetics.
- Outer modal containers, spotlight panels, and drawers expand to `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`).
- Fully rounded pills are explicitly avoided to preserve structural sobriety, except for microscopic notification counts and active filter dot indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid rust background (`#C85A32`), optical white text, 4px border radius, medium Inter font (`13px`). Hover state: darken to `#B34C27`.
- **Secondary (Editorial)**: Deep forest green background (`#1B382B`), warm paper text (`#F9F8F5`). Hover: `#122B22`.
- **Outline / Flat**: 1px border (`#E2DDD3`), paper-card background (`#FFFFFF`), text in `#1B382B`. Hover state reveals light canvas wash (`#F9F8F5`).

### Entity Badges & Chips
- Compact, durable tags featuring a 4px corner radius, 11px uppercase/semi-bold Inter typography, and 4px 8px padding.
- Automatically colored according to the entity schema (Startup, Person, Partner/VC, Provider, Programme).
- May include a subtle prefix dot (4px circle) displaying the entity category tint.

### Data Tables & Ecosystem Records
- Crisp white rows bordered by hairline `#E2DDD3` rules.
- Header row rests on subtle canvas `#F4F1EA` with all-caps 11px labels in `#60675D`.
- Alternating hover states apply `#F9F8F5`.
- Dedicated columns support monetary valuations, verification flags, and entity pills with right-aligned numeric data.

### Input Fields & Filter Bars
- Inputs feature a 1px border in `#E2DDD3`, white fill, 4px radius, and 8px 12px padding.
- Focus state switches border to `#1B382B` accompanied by a 1px solid ring.
- Integrated searchbars in headers feature clear monospaced shortcut indicators (e.g. `[ ⌘K ]`).

### Research Cards & Dossiers
- Background is clean `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border in `#E2DDD3`.
- Upper editorial frame contains display serif headlines (`Newsreader`), author metadata, and confidence scores.
- Lower section houses structured Inter tabular splits, tagged entity connections, and reference links.
