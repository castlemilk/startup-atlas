## Decisions that must survive integration

- **Country scope:** Nium has Singapore/San Francisco co-headquarters. Patsnap's 2022 company statement identifies Singapore, London and Toronto; its present HQ remains pending. Trax's Singapore HQ evidence is historical (2017). Carousell and ShopBack sources say regional HQ. AMILI and Glints use explicitly qualified public company-profile self-reports.
- **Incorporation:** Group/holding-company jurisdiction remains null for all 25 profiles. MAS explicitly establishes Singapore incorporation for four separately named operating entities: Coda Payments Pte. Ltd., Endowus Singapore Pte. Ltd., Syfe Pte. Ltd., and Asia Wealth Platform Pte. Ltd. Augmentus terms identify contracting entities with country labels, which is a different evidence scope.
- **Listed alumni:** Horizon Quantum's business combination completed on 19 March 2026 and Nasdaq trading began on 20 March. Its historical Series A is preserved in the alumni file; it is not among the 24 startup candidates.
- **Financing types:** Coda's US$690m transaction is a secondary share sale. Funding Societies' US$144m equity, US$150m debt lines and associated employee buyback are separate observations. bolttech's US$147m Series C share value has an unestablished primary/secondary split. No aggregate new-capital total is calculated.
- **Tranches and conversions:** ShopBack's US$200m Series F includes earlier tranches; Syfe's US$80m Series C includes US$27m C1 and US$53m C2. Endowus's total exceeds US$70m and includes US$20m fresh capital plus existing-note conversion; the new instrument and converted-note amount are not inferred. The API projection uses qualified amount text for the lower bound.
- **Split sources:** Trax's US$640m Series E includes primary and secondary transactions. Its numeric USD amount and announcement day are carried in separately sourced profile facts, because the undated company article does not independently establish both. The structured companion contains the unified, source-mapped observation.
- **Nulls and conflicts:** Glints' canonical founding year is null. SpeQtral's bare-dollar seed amount is not normalized to a currency. LionsBot factory spending is excluded from financing. Atomionics' 2019/2021 seed chronology is held as conflicting evidence. Eureka's 2024 announcement, 2025 webpage date and conflicting closing-year timeline remain distinct.
- **Round participants:** Every participation claim joins one event and one company, with source-named roles and unresolved canonical investor IDs. Nium's unnamed Series E lead stays unnamed; prior-round investors are not attached to that round. ShopBack's Asia Partners lead role is scoped to the initial tranche. Portfolio membership never creates a round edge.

The underlying source URLs and specific rejected interpretations are preserved in [decisions.json](decisions.json). No exact closing day is fabricated from an announcement.

The packet passes **27 semantic/provenance/coverage tests**, direct validation of **25 records** through the unchanged production validator, **seven negative schema fixtures**, the evidence skill's **468-claim validator**, the existing **31 company-profile planner/replay tests**, and the **10 graph tests** on refreshed main. Source reviews reread 15 URLs per lane, with overlap; they are bounded reviews, not a second exhaustive census.

A local, in-memory graph replay adds 24 candidate startup records to the pinned source inputs without modifying generated snapshots. It finds no funding-ID collision and yields 70 startup nodes. **The initial graph builder substituted `0` when `founded_year` was omitted. The existing owner independently fixed this in [PR #179](https://github.com/castlemilk/startup-atlas/pull/179), which merged while this packet was being validated.** The isolated branch was refreshed to that main revision. Final replay correctly omits the year for all 14 affected records, including Glints' true unknown and known years held in separately sourced profile facts. There are no zero sentinels. The original replay is retained as an audit checkpoint; no shared implementation change originates in the Singapore PR.

The baseline distinction matters: the committed graph is stale (537 entities / 17 rounds), while rebuilding current main source inputs gives **555 entities / 46 startups / 25 rounds**. Deduplication uses the rebuilt 46-startup set and the 45-entry overlay. No proposed company matches an existing name, alias, domain or startup ID.
