# Indonesia startup ecosystem: initial evidence checkpoint

**Research date:** 18 November 2026. **Packet:** `id-20261118-batch001`. **Status:** draft research for review; this report makes no claim of a push, pull request, merge or production publication.

## Scope and cohort

The initial checkpoint accepts **23 company profiles into staging**. Acceptance establishes documented Indonesia ecosystem relevance, not present private-company status, uninterrupted operations, complete founder lists or complete financing histories. The candidate ledger contains **39 discovered candidates: 24 reviewed, comprising 23 accepted profiles and one listed-company context record; 15 remain unreviewed; zero are rejected**. Fore Coffee is the separate listed context record. These are bounded research counts, not an estimate of Indonesia's startup population.

The assembled packet contains **505 claims**, **148 source observations across 147 distinct registered URLs**, 52 founder records, 29 capital events and 26 projected funding records. Eight claims retain conflict status. The distinction between capital events and projected funding records matters: a share transfer, receivables conversion, facility proposal and equity round cannot be treated as interchangeable fundraising observations. These counts are recorded in [manifest.json](manifest.json); company scope is in [company-index.json](company-index.json).

The [evidence ledger](evidence-ledger.json) preserves source URLs, retrieval dates, source-specific assertions and uncertainty alongside the usable profiles in [records.json](records.json). A successfully retrieved source can still be historically dated, incomplete or internally inconsistent. Company offices are not promoted to headquarters; named operating entities do not establish incorporation jurisdictions. Founding observations retain their available precision and distinguish business origins, product launches and group formation. Public professional founder names and source-scoped roles are included without collecting private contacts. The 52 founder records describe this batch's evidence coverage and should not be interpreted as exhaustive teams or independently resolved people across every historical entity.

**“GDP 17/30” is the user's supplied cohort label.** The official [October 2026 WEO report](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/weo/2026/october/english/text.pdf) verifies the requested edition (`ID-E041`). The supplied [NGDPD API](https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/api/v1/NGDPD/2026) failed, and the [WEO DataMapper page](https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/datasets/WEO) supplied no usable values (`ID-E001–002`). Indonesia's nominal GDP value and rank therefore remain unknown in this packet. The intended cohort uses 2026 estimates, excludes aggregates and treats Taiwan according to IMF's separately reported economy convention; no alternative vintage or real-growth table has been substituted.

## Geography: several complementary pathways

The sources support an analytical distinction between commercial networks around Jakarta/Jabodetabek, university technology pipelines elsewhere in Java and regional founder-development programmes. This is not a measured ranking of startup hubs.

**Jakarta and Jabodetabek.** [HUB.ID](https://www.hub.id/about/) describes corporate, startup and investor matchmaking (`ID-E005`). BRIN's [10 November 2026 announcement](https://www.brin.go.id/press-release/131380/brin-luncurkan-rumah-inovasi-indonesia-percepat-hilirisasi-dan-pemanfaatan-inovasi-nasional) reports the launch of Rumah Inovasi Indonesia in South Jakarta to connect research, industry, investors and universities (`ID-E034`). In Tangerang, [UMN's Skystar Ventures](https://skystarventures.com/program/incubation-program/) describes incubation through ideation, growth and later development (`ID-E030`). Jabodetabek should remain geographically explicit: a Tangerang or BSD operating site is not automatically Jakarta headquarters.

**Bandung.** ITB identifies its [Ganesha and Teknopolis innovation parks](https://dhkt.itb.ac.id/id/layanan/inkubasi-dan-akselerasi-bisnis) as channels for technology incubation, business validation, mentoring and industry/investor connections (`ID-E019`). Its [January 2026 PRIME STeP call](https://dhkt.itb.ac.id/id/berita/detail/open-registration-inkubasi-akselerasi-startup-prime-step-2026) distinguishes incubation and acceleration criteria (`ID-E020`). Institutional affiliation supplies a discovery pathway; it does not establish an affiliated company's current headquarters or investment history.

**Yogyakarta.** [UGM Innovative Academy](https://ia.ugm.ac.id/programs/) identifies health/pharmacy, agroindustry, renewable energy, engineering/digital technology and cultural heritage as innovation areas (`ID-E025`). Its reviewed [2025 startup grant](https://ia.ugm.ac.id/startup-grant-2025-innovative-academy-ugm-telah-dibuka/) had a 16–30 June 2025 application window (`ID-E026`). JALA provides a company-level example of regional coverage: its [official page](https://jala.tech/id/about) labels the Sleman site as headquarters and distinguishes its Surabaya business office (`re-jala-about`).

**Surabaya.** [ITS's incubator and Science Techno Park](https://www.its.ac.id/stp/inkubator/) identify ICT/robotics, maritime, automotive/transport and creative design pathways (`ID-E021`). The institution's aggregate marketing counts were not converted into a current startup denominator. Named tenant profiles are the next appropriate enumeration unit.

**Bali.** The reviewed [HUB.ID homepage](https://www.hub.id/) advertises a Bali summit for 20–21 October 2026 (`ID-E006`); a past advertised date does not independently prove completion. [Primakara's August 2026 account](https://inbis.primakara.ac.id/en/news/78) documents discussions about a planned Garuda Spark hub (`ID-E038`), not proof that the hub was operational. Separately, [Telkom's February 2025 release](https://www.telkom.co.id/sites/berita/id_ID/news/intip-cara-telkom-perkuat-ekosistem-ai-melalui-indigohub-dan-indigospace-2869) provides actual historical regional AI-activity evidence, including Denpasar (`ID-E037`).

**Beyond Java.** Garuda Spark's [September 2026 announcement](https://garudaspark.id/2026/09/01/gsih-hubungkan-10-kota-meutya-hafid-pastikan-inovasi-daerah-terhubung-dengan-talenta-investor-dan-pasar-global/) reports ten locations launched on 26 August, including Batam, Medan, Makassar, Papua and Aceh (`ID-E035`). These labels mix cities and provinces. Its [undated homepage](https://garudaspark.id/) describes twenty regional nodes (`ID-E028`); planned network scale and dated launches remain separate observations. Regional company coverage outside Java is still thin.

## Public programmes and accelerators

[1000 Startup Digital](https://1000startupdigital.id/program/) presents a five-stage formation pathway from ideas to MVP development and mentoring (`ID-E003`). Its programme page says twenty cities, while its [registration page](https://dashboard.1000startupdigital.id/register) says seventeen (`ID-E033`). Neither number is selected as the current truth. Zero counters returned elsewhere on the site are rendering artefacts, not evidence of zero participants.

[Startup Studio Indonesia](https://startupstudio.id/) presents an SSI x IBM 2026 programme covering product/market validation, product-market fit, governance and financial compliance, with an April–August main programme period (`ID-E008`). This is evidence of programme design and advertised timing; **November applications are not verified open**. HUB.ID's advertised Jakarta business-development period and Bali summit similarly describe scheduled activity, not confirmed outcomes or investments.

The ministry's [BEKUP 2025 launch](https://ekraf.go.id/news/menteri-ekraf-buka-kick-off-bekup-2025-gerakkan-inovasi-startup-dan-buka-lapangan-kerja) supplies a creative-economy startup pathway (`ID-E007`). BRIN's [RIIM Startup announcement](https://www.brin.go.id/press-release/119840/brin-siap-fasilitasi-pendanaan-startup-berbasis-riset-hingga-rp-300-juta-per-tahun) described support up to IDR300 million annually in 2024 (`ID-E018`); this historical cap is neither a company award nor a verified 2026 entitlement.

[Antler Indonesia](https://www.antler.co/location/indonesia) describes a six-week, in-person Jakarta process with investment conditional on due diligence (`ID-E017`). The separately reviewed [cohort table](https://www.antler.co/cohort-start-dates) supplied no next Indonesia date (`ID-E022`). Programme participation, incubator membership and an institution's address never establish automatic funding or applicant eligibility.

## Sectors and capital networks

The accepted sample spans payments, credit, investment platforms, enterprise software, commerce infrastructure, health, education, travel, seafood supply chains, logistics and waste management. These categories describe the sample, not sector market shares. Research commercialization and industrial innovation are less represented than venture-backed digital services; university cohorts are therefore a useful next source family.

[East Ventures](https://east.vc/id/tentang-east-ventures) describes sector-agnostic, multistage Southeast Asian investing (`ID-E036`). Its [2026 digital-competitiveness release](https://east.vc/id/berita/press-release/east-ventures-releases-digital-competitiveness-index-2026) places DKI Jakarta, West Java, East Java, Banten and DI Yogyakarta first through fifth (`ID-E027`). That is regional digital-readiness context, not a startup-count or venture-funding ranking.

The former AC Ventures about URL redirects to [ACV Capital](https://acvcap.com/about/), whose current page describes growth equity (`ID-E011`). Historical manager/fund identity must be reviewed before canonical joins. [Alpha JWC](https://www.alphajwc.com/portfolios/) explicitly presents a curated cross-border portfolio (`ID-E013`); [Intudo](https://intudovc.com/manifesto/) combines Indonesia/Silicon Valley platform language with Indonesia-focused investment wording (`ID-E023`). Neither is a national census.

[BRI Ventures](https://www.briventures.id/about) and [MDI Ventures](https://mdi.vc/about) provide corporate-network discovery routes (`ID-E015–016`). MDI describes multiple funds and third-party LPs: manager, fund, parent and LP remain separate identities. BRI's duplicated Sembrani timeline gives conflicting 2020/2021 launch years. **Portfolio membership alone is never evidence of participation in a particular financing event.**

- [IFC's Evermos release](https://www.ifc.org/en/pressroom/2023/27547) reports IFC and EAF contributions (`id-core-evermos-ifc`). They are components of the company-reported financing, not additional rounds. Earlier proposed limits remain distinct from later invested amounts.
