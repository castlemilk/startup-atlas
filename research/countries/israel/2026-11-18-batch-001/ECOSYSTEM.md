## Ecosystem structure: concentrated technology, international company structures

The [Israel Innovation Authority’s 2026 report](https://innovationisrael.org.il/en/press_release/israeli-high-tech-report-2026/) describes approximately `$14.6 billion` raised in 2025 and about 775 newly established startups. Cybersecurity, enterprise software and fintech accounted for over 60% of capital raised under its definitions; by March 2026, 62% of employees of private Israeli companies were located in Israel. These are dated national-report measures, not a census generated by this packet. The [Hebrew edition](https://innovationisrael.org.il/press_release/innovation-report-2026/) was also checked, but both editions belong to the same report family.

[Startup Nation Central’s earlier estimate](https://startupnationcentral.org/hub/news/pr-israeli-tech-annual-report-2025/) put 2025 funding at `$15.6 billion`, using information and estimates available on December 17, 2025. The different cutoff and methodology prevent treating the two totals as directly interchangeable. Both amounts retain their published dollar notation here; neither becomes a summed batch funding measure.

The company evidence suggests two useful research dimensions. First, Israel-linked ventures span enterprise software and scientific or industrial technologies. Second, international headquarters, Israeli research offices, founding origins and legal entities frequently differ. This is an inference from the reviewed sample, not an estimate of national proportions. [CropX’s New Zealand origin and subsequent move to Israel](https://cropx.com/about/company/), [Island’s Dallas headquarters and Tel Aviv R&D](https://www.island.io/press/island-introduces-first-enterprise-browser-to-radically-improve-enterprise-security-and-redefine-the-nature-of-work), and [Immunai’s New York headquarters and Israeli office](https://www.immunai.com/company/) demonstrate why a single country field would lose material information.

### Sector interpretation

The research deliberately allocated eight candidates to each of three broad workstreams. Security companies cover data protection, enterprise networking, identity, cloud risk and security operations; the climate/health workstream covers cultivated food, fermentation, agricultural sensing, robotics, therapeutic equipment and clinical/drug-discovery AI; the AI/quantum workstream spans language models, code review, avatars, voice, chips and quantum control/software. The table below supplies company-level evidence for those descriptions.

This balanced selection is a research design, not an ecosystem sector distribution. Fintech, consumer products, telecoms, mobility, space, materials and many university spinouts require additional batches. Hardware and biological ventures also need transaction classifications that can accommodate strategic corporate participation, public support and mixed financing without treating every relationship as equity.

Three acquisition holds prevent stale startup classification. Founding and publication conflicts remain visible. These are preserved observations, not silently repaired timestamps.

Further retrieval stopped when [Pentera’s terms](https://pentera.io/legal-hub/terms-and-conditions/) and [Deepdub’s terms](https://deepdub.ai/legal/terms-of-use) exposed automated-access restrictions. Hailo’s English announcement returned 403; an independently discoverable official language-route page supplied the reviewed release. A Craft article redirected away from its supporting content, a Deepdub PDF had no reviewed extractable content, and several date-section reads failed. Successful empty responses do not support claims. Immunai’s [rendered zero counters](https://www.immunai.com/company/) were rejected as numeric facts, while its [moving resource pagination](https://www.immunai.com/resources/page/7/) shows why page numbers are unsuitable event identifiers.

The sample favours visible companies with accessible announcements, English-language material and disclosed funding. A Hebrew national-report edition broadens language coverage but does not make company research multilingual or representative. Smaller, undisclosed, bootstrapped and regionally based ventures are likely underrepresented; this is a research-design limitation, not a measured estimate. Seventeen records have some HQ observation, including historical or regional labels; seven do not. Twenty-three have non-null founding observations; one is held. Eleven have some legal-entity information, but only six establish incorporation country. Unknown does not mean zero, absent or ineligible.
