### Sectors and capital signals

Software, AI, developer tools, data and marketplaces appear in the declared strategies of [Inovo](https://inovo.vc/), [Market One Capital](https://www.moc.vc/investment-profile), [SMOK](https://www.smok.vc/faq/) and [Innovation Nest](https://innovationnest.com/fund). Industrial technology, climate applications and circular economy have distinct programme routes through [KPT](https://www.kpt.krakow.pl/nasze-projekty/), [PPNT](https://ppnt.poznan.pl/akcelerator-credit4ce-wesprzemy-20-startupow/) and [Startup Spark](https://sse.lodz.pl/projekty/spark/). Space, robotics, cybersecurity and dual-use technology appear in [OTB’s focus](https://otb.vc/portfolio/) and [PFR’s Deep Tech programme](https://pfrventures.pl/en/funds). These are source-selection guides, not national sector percentages.

The [PFR Ventures/Inovo Q3 2026 release](https://pfrventures.pl/artykul/transakcje-na-polskim-rynku-vc-w-q3-2026), published on 21 October 2026, reports PLN 407 million for that quarter and PLN 5.03 billion for the first nine months, with the latter materially affected by ElevenLabs and ICEYE megarounds. These are the report’s aggregate observations; they are not a sum of this packet’s verified completed financings, and they do not define a Poland-headquartered startup population. Its [2025 report](https://pfrventures.pl/artykul/transakcje-na-polskim-rynku-vc-w-2025) describes SaaS as the most common business model among its observed financed companies and records health/biotechnology activity. That historical financed-company sample must not be presented as the sector mix of all Polish startups.

## Evidence decisions that materially affect interpretation

**Company financing, liquidity and conditional announcements remain separate.** ElevenLabs’ employee tender is employee secondary liquidity. Nomagic’s 2025 reported total includes previously signed venture debt. Synerise’s 2022 transaction includes primary capital and secondary share acquisition. ICEYE’s June 2026 transaction separates a primary component from a larger combined transaction and is explicitly conditional. SatRev’s 2021 announcement does not prove final closing. Those observations remain in the sidecar with their semantics and are omitted from the existing simple funding projection. Sources are linked in the company table.

**Investor contributions do not become round totals.** BioCam’s Level2 Ventures ticket is retained as a participation-level amount; the batch does not invent the size of the full round. Nor does it treat earlier investors mentioned in the same article as participants in the later event. [Level2 announcement](https://level2.vc/news/biocam-has-raised-pln-2m-from-level2-ventures-for-further-development-of-its-ai-powered-endoscopic-capsule.html).

**Per-claim attribution takes precedence over a convenient company-wide source.** Founder, office, legal-entity, product, round and participant claims may have different sources and observation dates. Autenti’s existing shareholders become round participants only because the full [Series B announcement](https://autenti.com/en/blog/new-investors-join-autentis-eur-8.5m-round-as-it-scales-rapidly-across-europe) explicitly ties them to round B. An investor portfolio card alone would not do that.

**Uncertainty is retained rather than resolved by guesswork.** Booksy’s adjacent announcement dates remain a day-level conflict; Ramp’s numeric date is not forced to a locale; Brainly’s founder spelling conflict is not an automatic person-identity merge. Company foundation, product launch, incorporation and legal-form conversion remain distinct events. A missing amount, currency, close, founder profile or alias is not a zero, a negative finding or proof that the list is complete.
