## Ecosystem structure

### Stockholm and the surrounding region

Stockholm supplies the most visible concentration in this first pass: AI software, fintech, enterprise data software, mobility, preventive health and energy orchestration. This is a discovery advantage and a sampling bias. The regional publisher's valuation and unicorn report uses its own geographic and company definitions; its totals are not the denominator for the Atlas batch. [Stockholm Business Region, 9 March 2026](https://en.stockholmbusinessregion.se/news/33-unicorns-and-252-billion-dollars/).

The institutional routes are distinct. [Sting](https://www.sting.co/faqs/what-is-sting) provides incubation and acceleration across climate, health and digital society. It [accepts applicants across Sweden](https://www.sting.co/faqs/do-i-need-to-be-based-in-stockholm), with attendance expectations in Stockholm; programme membership therefore does not establish headquarters. [KTH Innovation](https://www.kth.se/en/om/innovation/om/innovationskraft-pa-kth-1.504834) supplies free, confidential, no-equity support to its university community, while KTH Ventures is a separate investor. [SSE Business Lab](https://www.hhs.se/community/sse-business-lab/) connects students, alumni and faculty with incubation, coaching and investor networks; SSE Ventures should likewise remain a separate investment identity.

The [SSE summer 2026 cohort](https://www.hhs.se/news/sse/2026/eight-new-startups-join-sse-business-labs-summer-batch/) is an unusually useful next enumeration unit: eight named companies are published in a finite list. Their names have been registered, but their domains, legal entities, founders and funding are not yet accepted. This gives a defensible denominator of eight for that source cohort only.

### Gothenburg and western Sweden

[Chalmers Ventures](https://chalmersventures.com/) combines research commercialization, venture building and deeptech investment. The reviewed company's own description emphasizes university spinouts and commercial development from research. Its [portfolio directory](https://chalmersventures.com/startups/) distinguishes current holdings, alumni, exits and listed companies, so a complete directory import without those distinctions would produce misleading current-startup classifications.

ZeroPoint Technologies and Elypta connect this pass to research commercialization, while transport, energy and hardware remain important follow-up themes. Heart Aerospace illustrates the geographic problem: its Swedish origin is retained, but its official announcement says the corporate HQ moved to Los Angeles and Swedish operations were being closed. It is held from the current Sweden-profile batch. [Heart relocation announcement](https://www.heartaerospace.com/newsroom/heart-aerospace-relocates-corporate-headquarters-to-los-angeles-california).

### Malmö–Lund and southern Sweden

[Minc](https://www.minc.se/about) is a Malmö city-owned hub offering incubation, acceleration and investor connections, with regional games activity alongside other ventures. Its [startup/alumni directory](https://www.minc.se/community/startup-alumnis) supplies discovery leads but mixes statuses. DBTune, Dockai and Edda Knowledge are queued; none receives accepted company fields merely from directory membership.

[SmiLe Venture Hub](https://www.smileventurehub.com/about-smile/) provides the complementary life-science path in Lund, including laboratory and commercialization support at Medicon Village. It describes a nonprofit, no-equity model. The next batch should follow company-owned and university-origin sources for biotech, diagnostics and medtech, keeping programme support separate from investment.

### Uppsala

[Uppsala Innovation Centre](https://uic.se/en/om-oss/) offers staged commercialization programmes and business coaching. Its network should be explored alongside university-origin disclosures and the official company directory, with programme-specific eligibility checked on the corresponding pages. Altris is accepted in this batch, with operations and legal incorporation distinguished. Its founder record also preserves the difference between a three-researcher company narrative and six founders named by the originating university; the shorter narrative is not treated as a complete founder list.

### Linköping–Norrköping

[LEAD](https://lead.se/en/about-us/), owned by LiU Holding, provides the regional incubation route. Epishine brings printed solar-cell hardware into the initial company set. The LEAD page contains differing inception dates, so this packet accepts the institutional role and locations without silently selecting an institutional founding year. Regional deeptech, industrial software and energy companies remain next-pass priorities.

### Umeå and northern Sweden

[Uminova Innovation](https://www.uminovainnovation.se/sok-startupprogrammet/) supports early idea validation in Umeå, with a [directory separating current companies and alumni](https://www.uminovainnovation.se/bolag/). Its displayed October 2026 programme start is historical at the retrieval date; it is not represented as a current open application window. Biodiv, Emendion and Gnistergy are selected discovery leads.

[Arctic Business](https://abi.se/about-us/) operates from Luleå, Piteå and Skellefteå, with space, mining and energy themes and ESA BIC Sweden activity. Northern research should include small university and industrial spinouts as well as large capital-intensive projects. Northvolt's bankruptcy and later asset acquisition illustrate why a northern industrial asset base cannot be equated with an unchanged active-startup identity. [Northvolt bankruptcy](https://northvolt.com/articles/northvolt-files-for-bankruptcy-in-sweden/); [Lyten acquisition of Swedish assets](https://lyten.com/2026/02/26/lyten-completes-acquisition-of-northvolt-sweden-and-establishes-its-first-lyten-industrial-hub-in-sweden/).
