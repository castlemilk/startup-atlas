## Cohort, scope and evidence rules

The session uses the user's **IMF October 2026 WEO, 2026 nominal GDP estimates** cohort label, **19/30**. The official [WEO dataset page](https://data.imf.org/datasets/WEO) identifies the October 2026 vintage. The requested [DataMapper API](https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/api/v1/NGDPD/2026) was unavailable to the research tool, while the [DataMapper WEO page](https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/datasets/WEO) returned a JavaScript shell. No GDP value or country ordering was recomputed. The rank remains a supplied cohort label; an estimate is not presented as a final actual. Aggregates are excluded in the user's cohort definition, and Taiwan follows the IMF's separately reported economy convention.

Country inclusion means a documented Saudi headquarters, operating or founding connection, or an explicitly identified Saudi ecosystem connection. It does **not** imply Saudi incorporation. The records distinguish historical observations from verified current facts, company formation from launch, parent entities from operating companies, and operating locations from headquarters. Data on founders is limited to public professional identities and roles; no personal contact dataset is included.

Every accepted or conflicting claim retains its source URL, retrieval timestamp and its precision, publication/effective dates where known, evidence paraphrase, source kind, acquisition method, confidence, rationale and disposition. `inputs/` preserves the four researcher snapshots. `review-corrections.json` records post-packet classification changes: Lean's Riyadh base and Polymeron's Thuwal base are operating locations, with headquarters unknown. Sources using an unqualified dollar sign do not receive an invented ISO currency. Month-only and year-only dates are never padded to an artificial day. Missing amounts remain null in the lossless ledger and omitted from schema fields that cannot accept null.

[TAQADAM](https://taqadam.kaust.edu.sa/); [Sadeem](https://sadeemwss.com/our-story/); [Polymeron company profile](https://www.kaust.edu.sa/en/news/turning-date-waste-into--green--plastic--kaust-startup-polymeron-champions-circular-economy).

## Public institutions perform different functions

**Saudi Venture Capital (SVC)** describes itself as an investment company established in 2018, now a subsidiary of SME Bank under the National Development Fund. Its mandate includes investment in funds and direct investment supporting startups and SMEs. **Jada**, established by PIF in 2018, describes a fund-of-funds role across venture capital, private equity and private debt. For Atlas, these are distinct capital relationships: a commitment to a manager’s fund does not establish participation in a particular portfolio company’s financing. [SVC mandate](https://svc.com.sa/en/about-us/); [Jada mandate](https://www.jada.com.sa/about-us).

**Fintech Saudi** states that SAMA and the Capital Market Authority launched the initiative in April 2018 to develop the fintech ecosystem, including infrastructure, talent and founder support. Programme membership, directory presence and regulatory permission require separate evidence. A later fintech enumeration should distinguish ecosystem members, service providers, sandbox participants and licensed firms, using the relevant regulator’s own record for authorization status. [Fintech Saudi history](https://fintechsaudi.sa/en-us/Pages/OurStory.aspx).

**NTDP/NextEra** is supported here through a specific deployment case: KAUST reports that NextEra support helped Polymeron acquire pilot-plant equipment. The NTDP portal yielded no readable body during this pass, so the packet does not generalize that case into current grant amounts, eligibility rules or application windows. [KAUST NextEra case](https://www.kaust.edu.sa/en/news/turning-date-waste-into--green--plastic--kaust-startup-polymeron-champions-circular-economy); [NTDP portal](https://ntdp.mcit.gov.sa/).

## Interpretation and continuation

The deeptech sample exposes practical classification risks. Uvera’s KAUST profile reports a **California founding in 2019**, while Morgan Stanley’s 2025 cohort labels the company **Saudi Arabia**; current headquarters and incorporation remain unresolved.
