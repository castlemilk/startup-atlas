## Institutional structure and current changes

Norway's reviewed sources expose several connected routes to company formation: university commercialisation, regional incubation, industrial clusters, public instruments and private investment. This is a research map of those routes, not a measurement of their relative economic weight. An incubator membership, an investor portfolio position and a particular financing event are separate relationships throughout the packet.

Three changes materially affect a current ecosystem map. Innovation Norway took over the incubation and business-garden programmes from Siva **effective 1 April 2026**. Siva's earlier programme pages remain historical evidence; Siva continues to administer Norsk katapult industrial testing infrastructure. GCE Ocean Technology now describes a combined Energy Cluster Norway structure with Energy Transition Norway and Energy Valley. Antler's old Norway route redirects to a Nordic page describing an inception-stage VC model. These require temporal and organisational relationships rather than silent replacement of names. [Programme transfer](https://www.innovasjonnorge.no/nyhetsartikkel/na-er-naeringshager-og-inkubatorer-flyttet-fra-siva-til-innovasjon-norge); [Norsk katapult](https://siva.no/virkemidler/norsk-katapult/); [GCE Ocean Technology](https://www.gceocean.no/about-us/); [Antler Nordics](https://www.antler.co/nordics).

## Public programmes and commercialisation support

Innovation Norway's **Oppstartstilskudd 1** supports market validation. The accessed page gives a maximum of NOK 150,000 and conditions including an innovative, growth-oriented AS younger than five years. **Oppstartslån** supports commercial launch through debt, with the accessed page describing a NOK 2 million company cap. **Innovasjonskontrakter** supports development with a pilot customer and international potential. These are snapshots of published terms on the evidence date; full eligibility, available budgets and application timing require a fresh check for an actual applicant. [Grant](https://www.innovasjonnorge.no/tjeneste/oppstartstilskudd-1); [loan](https://www.innovasjonnorge.no/tjeneste/oppstartslan); [innovation contracts](https://www.innovasjonnorge.no/tjeneste/tilskudd-til-innovasjonskontrakter).

**SkatteFUNN** is an R&D tax incentive administered through Forskningsrådet and the tax authority. Its current page describes a 19% deduction and labels changes from 2027 as proposals. The packet preserves proposal status and does not treat the proposed terms as enacted. An R&D project approval, eligible costs, a tax incentive and cash equity are different facts. [SkatteFUNN](https://www.forskningsradet.no/skattefunn/).

The incubation programme funds innovation environments operating incubators; startup access is mediated through those operators. **Norsk katapult** provides industrial testing and prototyping infrastructure. It is distinct from the private **Katapult** investment ecosystem, despite the similar name. Public support must be recorded by its actual instrument and recipient, without converting operator grants or company subsidies into equity-round participants. [Incubation programme](https://www.innovasjonnorge.no/en/tjeneste/inkubasjonsprogrammet); [Norsk katapult](https://siva.no/virkemidler/norsk-katapult/); [Katapult group](https://katapult.vc/ocean/ocean-accelerator/).

The financing records apply this distinction. Ocean Oasis's company announcement discusses a capital raise and an EIC grant separately. Hystar's Series C announcement also refers to Innovation Fund grant support. Plaace's investor announcement reports a NOK 10 million round and NOK 2 million of Innovation Norway support with an ambiguous relationship between the headline and support component. The packet preserves the ambiguity; it does not manufacture a NOK 12 million total or make Innovation Norway an equity participant. [Ocean Oasis](https://www.oceanoasis.co/news/raises-capital/); [Hystar](https://hystar.com/hystar-raises-usd-36m-to-accelerate-commercial-growth/); [Plaace announcement](https://runwayfbu.com/blog/meet-our-newest-investment-proptech-plaace).

## Investor and accelerator networks

- **Investinor** distinguishes direct investment, investments in seed/venture funds, matching and pre-seed routes. A fund commitment does not establish a direct investment in every underlying startup. [Investinor](https://investinor.no/om-oss/).
- **Nysnø** describes minority climate investment alongside private investors. Its geographic mandate includes operations in, or origin from, Norway; the portfolio mixes companies and funds and cannot be used as a Norwegian-HQ census. [Strategy](https://nysnoinvest.no/en/strategy/); [portfolio](https://nysnoinvest.no/en/portfolio/).
- **Startuplab** combines incubation, acceleration and early investment. **Antler** identifies Oslo among four Nordic hubs. Programme participation and a financing event need distinct evidence even when one operator provides both. [Startuplab](https://www.startuplab.no/); [Antler](https://www.antler.co/nordics).
- **Alliance VC** provides a Nordic portfolio with active/exited controls. Nysnø's Alliance Nordic III page describes an Oslo manager and a predominantly seed strategy with some pre-seed activity. The manager, fund and startup are different entities. [Portfolio](https://alliance.vc/portfolio/); [fund evidence](https://nysnoinvest.no/en/case/alliance-nordic-iii/).
- **SNÖ Ventures** provides company discovery, but its displayed co-investors are undated and cannot be assigned to particular rounds. **Idékapital** describes scalable software/technology businesses. **Verdane** supplies a European growth perspective spanning digitalisation and decarbonisation, with an Oslo office. [SNÖ](https://sno.vc/); [Idékapital](https://idekapital.com/); [Verdane](https://verdane.com/).
- **Katapult Ocean** identifies Oslo as global headquarters while investing internationally; each portfolio company's country relationship needs its own review. [Organisation](https://www.katapultocean.com/); [portfolio](https://www.katapultocean.com/portfolio).

Northzone is a useful geography example. The official register lists NORTHZONE VENTURES NORWAY AS at an Oslo business address, while the global About page advertises five offices outside Norway. A Norwegian legal entity and the global firm's advertised offices can coexist without establishing Norwegian global headquarters. [Norwegian entity](https://virksomhet.brreg.no/nb/oppslag/enheter/895407992); [global organisation](https://www.northzone.com/about).

## Methods, provenance and integration boundary

Research used bounded search and page reads, prioritising official company, investor, programme, university and registry material, including Norwegian-language releases. Search snippets served as leads. Claims retain source URLs, retrieval dates, available publication/effective dates, uncertainty and relationship scope. Founding, incorporation, announcement, closing and page-update dates remain distinct; professional founder information excludes personal contact details. Unknowns remain null or explicitly unresolved, rather than zero, false or invented dates.

The ecosystem lane accessed 38 distinct source URLs and retained 46 reviewed institutional claims before assembly with the company lanes. Those are lane-level evidence counts, not a count of unique sources across the entire packet. The final machine-readable manifests and validation results are the authority for packet-wide file, claim, relationship and test counts. No directory census, full founder list, complete funding history or nationwide completeness assertion follows from this checkpoint.
