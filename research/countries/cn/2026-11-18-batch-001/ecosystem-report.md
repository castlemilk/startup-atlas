# China startup ecosystem — bounded research batch 001

**Research date:** 18 November 2026. **Cohort label:** GDP 2/30. **Economy:** China, People’s Republic of (`CHN`, `CN`). **Status:** staged evidence packet, separate from production.

This checkpoint accepts 18 company identities for a source-backed research packet drawn from a selected worklist of 22. It maps research institutions, operating locations, hardware infrastructure, public programmes and investment relationships around those companies. Acceptance establishes evidence-ready membership of this packet; it does not certify a complete profile, current private-company status, investment eligibility, production acceptance or publication.

The observed network connects Beijing’s research institutions and model developers, Shanghai’s AI and robotics companies, Shenzhen and Dongguan’s robotics and engineering infrastructure, Hangzhou’s AI and robotics activity, and distributed aerospace manufacturing sites. These are findings from deliberately selected sources, not a ranking of hubs or an estimate of their shares of China’s startup population. The underlying evidence includes the [Beijing science-city profile](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/beijinginfo/sci/innovationservices/resources/sciencecitiesandbda/202601/t20260115_4432661.html), [Shanghai’s Zhangjiang plan](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Latest-WhatsNew/20261027/138eb7e92d9b443ba87ba02921ca98f4.html), [XbotPark’s account of its Dongguan model](https://www.xbotpark.com/aboutus/), [Hangzhou contextual reporting](https://www.ehangzhou.gov.cn/2026-03/10/c_296992.htm) and the company location sources below.

## 1. Cohort, boundaries and measured scope

The [IMF WEO dataset landing page](https://data.imf.org/datasets/WEO) confirmed the **October 2026 WEO** vintage. The cohort uses 2026 nominal GDP estimates, `NGDPD`, expressed in billions of US dollars; these are estimates, not final actuals. The requested [DataMapper API](https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/api/v1/NGDPD/2026) did not return a usable JSON body. The [legacy WEO page](https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/datasets/WEO) and [China profile](https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/profile/CHN) exposed application templates rather than usable exact values. Accordingly, **China’s exact GDP and country-only rank were not independently recomputed**. “2/30” remains the user-assigned session position.

The country scope follows the selected IMF economy keys: aggregates are excluded; Taiwan follows the separately reported economy convention, and Hong Kong and Macao are separate economy keys. A Hong Kong investor, overseas holding company or international office does not automatically change a mainland operating company’s research association. Headquarters, registered jurisdiction, operating locations and listing venue are preserved as separate observations. Moonshot’s [investor interim report](https://doc.irasia.com/listco/hk/cmcdi/interim/2025/intrep.pdf), for example, describes a Cayman holding company alongside the [company’s Beijing contact office](https://www.moonshot.cn/about).
