[Accel's Anysphere/Cursor account](https://www.accel.com/news/spacex-cursor-it-is-so-so-early) reports acquisition by SpaceX. Both remain useful ecosystem alumni but are excluded from this independent-startup projection. Anthropic's [17 November preliminary prospectus](https://ipo.anthropic.com/documents/us/prospectus.pdf) is subject to completion; a proposed or approved listing does not establish completed trading. Other accepted companies are not labelled verified-private merely because no exit appeared in the reviewed sources.

**Amounts and currencies:** Unqualified dollar signs are retained as reported text without automatic USD conversion. “Over” and “more than” remain lower bounds. [Mercury's $300 million announcement](https://mercury.com/blog/series-c-announcement) combines primary and secondary transactions; it cannot be treated as $300 million of newly issued company capital. Undisclosed amounts remain unknown, not zero. Debt, grants, commercial contracts and valuations are separate from equity round proceeds.

**Dates and extensions:** Publication, announcement and closing dates remain distinct. [Helion's Series G](https://www.helionenergy.com/newsroom/helion-raises-465-million-series-g-funding-round-to-meet-surging-global-demand-for-power) is one financing updated from $465 million to $500 million, with final closing known only to September 2026. [Redwood's final Series E](https://www.redwoodmaterials.com/news/redwood-materials-announces-final-close-of-425m-series-e/) increases its earlier $350 million financing to $425 million. Summing initial and final totals would double count capital. Participant claims retain the announcement or close that actually names them.

**Founding and identity:** Hadrian and Zipline retain conflicting founding-year observations without an unqualified year. Company start dates are not incorporation dates. Founder lists are explicitly partial; public professional roles are dated when historical, and addresses or personal contacts are not harvested. Glean's verified operations do not resolve its headquarters or incorporation sufficiently for this batch's country decision.
