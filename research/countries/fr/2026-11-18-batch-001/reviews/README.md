The first three attach to the capital-increase component. The latter three remain context-only, with no assertion of a fresh subscription. The final named event-participation count is 187, not the intermediate 190.

Verkor's Macquarie vehicle is a descriptive translation, not a resolved formal fund name. Its July 2020 founding has separate Bpifrance corroboration. Mistral's projected valuation note preserves the post-money qualifier. These decisions appear in the normalized data and projection map.

The initial graph-builder year-zero issue was resolved by the existing owner's merged [PR #179](https://github.com/castlemilk/startup-atlas/pull/179) before final validation. It is not an active hold. The packet consumes that fix and introduces no implementation replacement.

Independent review means the stated subset of source pages and data relationships was checked. It does not mean every fact was independently reopened, every founder identified, every financing enumerated, or the country's startup population exhausted.
