## Scope, method and interpretation

The research combines official company histories, investor announcements, exchange information, public programmes, university commercialisation sources and accelerator directories. English and Traditional Chinese material was used, with Japanese corporate sources where relevant to cross-border groups. Each accepted claim retains its source URL, retrieval date, precision and uncertainty. A company-hosted press excerpt, company-maintained professional profile and direct regulator record have different evidentiary weight; hosting does not erase that distinction.

The repository baseline is pinned in [baseline-inventory.json](baseline-inventory.json). Normalized company names, canonical domains, aliases and existing entity identifiers are the starting points for deduplication. A product, former brand, token network, subsidiary and holding company are not automatically separate startups or the same legal entity. The packet's validation and deduplication results, together with computed discovered/reviewed/accepted/held/rejected counts, are recorded in [coverage.json](coverage.json). Those counts should be read with their stated unit and denominator.

Acceptance means that the record is sufficiently supported for **staging with explicit field limitations**. It does not mean that every requested field is known, that each source is current for every claim, or that the company has been published. Founding, incorporation, headquarters, operating locations, historical origin and present listing status are separately represented. Unknown amounts, dates, headcount and geography remain unknown; a missing field is not zero or proof of absence.

Directory membership supplies leads. It does not establish Taiwan headquarters, legal incorporation, accelerator investment or participation in a particular funding round. Research teams may precede incorporated companies, and global investors may include overseas businesses in Taiwan-facing portfolios. No country startup denominator was established, no directory was exhausted, and this batch does not claim national completeness.

## Public programmes and research commercialisation

**Startup Terrace**, under SMESA, provides facilities and industry connections in Linkou and Kaohsiung. **ITRI's incubation centre** supports technology ventures and joint R&D; its admission framework includes conditional access for foreign companies. [Startup Terrace](https://startupterrace.sme.gov.tw/en/cp.aspx?n=1667) [TW-ECO-003]; [ITRI](https://www.itri.org.tw/English/ListStyle.aspx?DisplayStyle=03&MmmID=1037400104734710064&SiteID=1) [TW-ECO-008]

- iKala's [Chunghwa Telecom announcement](https://www.cht.com.tw/zh-tw/home/cht/messages/2024/0326-1610) reports **more than USD 20.8 million** in the 2024 B+ financing. Preserve the lower bound and the original company's different wording; do not normalize an exact amount from it. [tw-review-s06]
- TMYTEK's [December 2024 release](https://tmytek.com/post/tmytek-completes-US40-million-series-b-funding) describes **USD 25 million incremental B+** and **USD 40 million aggregate Series B**. The aggregate is not another round. [tw-dt-s17]
- KKday's [December 2024 announcement](https://www.kkday.com/ja/corp/news_fundraising2412/) combines investments and credit facilities and uses an approximate dollar amount. The packet does not recast it as an exact equity-only round with an inferred stage. [TW-ROOT-004]

Partial dates retain their precision. TeamT5's later company history supports a **2022 investment year**, while its accessed Series A release is undated; neither establishes an exact close date.
