## Collection implications

South Korea's source landscape supports three complementary research routes: private investor and accelerator company pages; national public-programme selections; and regional research or incubation clusters. An investor-led programme, a public grant, a cluster tenancy and an equity funding event need separate relationships. The best next pass should deliberately combine these routes to avoid equating well-publicized Seoul AI companies with the whole national ecosystem.

## Public programmes and capital

[TIPS](https://www.mss.go.kr/site/ulsan/ex/bbs/View.do?bcIdx=1065302&cbIdx=254) links private-operator selection and investment with government support. Its 2026 call and [Scale-Up expansion announcement](https://www.mss.go.kr/site/eng/ex/bbs/View.do?bcIdx=1064364&cbIdx=244) are useful policy evidence, but announced capacity is not a completed cohort. Detailed current selections, operator accreditation and amendments remain to be read.

[K-Startup Grand Challenge](https://ksgc.global/grand-challenge) targets foreign-national CEOs entering Korea. Its 2026 application window ended in June, and a Korean company was not required at application. Therefore alumni status does not establish Korean headquarters or incorporation.

The [2025 Startup Korea Fund selection announcement](https://www.mss.go.kr/site/smba/ex/bbs/View.do?bcIdx=1061359&cbIdx=86) includes deep-tech/global, open-innovation and secondary-fund categories. Keep historical planned fund formation separate from invested capital, and secondary transactions separate from new primary capital.

[KVIC](https://www.kvic.or.kr/index.do) is particularly useful for resolving the capital chain: public fund-of-funds, private managers, named underlying funds and company-level transactions. An LP commitment must not create a direct startup-investor relationship.

The main [K-Bio Lab Hub announcement](https://mss.go.kr/site/eng/ex/bbs/View.do?bcIdx=1071534&cbIdx=244) sets completion and tenant selection in 2028 and support from 2029. Recording the proposed main hub as operational in 2026 would be a material temporal error.

## Accelerators and investor networks

[dcamp](https://dcamp.kr/en) offers a combination of workspace, acceleration, investment and networks. Its several activities require distinct evidence types rather than one generic investor edge.

[MARU's dated H2 2026 notice](https://asan-nanum.org/notice/2026-anf-maru-open-h2/) establishes historical incubation terms. Applications closed in August; the notice is not a final admitted-company list. The separate MARU homepage gave an insufficient extract and supports no accepted finding in this packet.

[SparkLabs Korea](https://sparklabs.co.kr/en/) provides a broad early-stage company-discovery channel. Its FAQ describes indicative investment terms, but those terms cannot fill missing round amounts for portfolio companies. [Primer](https://www.primer.kr/) also provides useful company examples, including acquired businesses; status and geography need company-specific checks.

[KDB NextRound](https://www.nextround.kr/programs/nextround) supplies dated investor-relations and networking events. Its use of “round” denotes an IR session, not automatically a financing transaction. [KVCA](https://www.kvca.or.kr/en/) supplies membership and statistical-report discovery, while individual firms' official announcements are still required for deal participation.
