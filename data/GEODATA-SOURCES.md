# Globe data sources

- `land.geojson`: [Natural Earth, 1:110m land polygons](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson), public domain. Coastlines are a simplified small-scale depiction.
- `map-locations.json`: operator-provided sources, reviewed 12 November 2026. Coordinates approximate each city centre and do not represent an exact office or headquarters. The list is deliberately incomplete and does not establish eligibility or a provider relationship.
