**As of:** 24 November 2026
**Research method:** Live web research (company newsroom, reputable press, regulator-related reporting). Every material claim is cited inline. Where a fact could not be established from a live source, it is marked "Not found".

## 1. Overview & founding story

- Founded **1 July 2015**, in **London, England, United Kingdom**, by **Nik Storonsky (also spelled Nikolay / Nik Storonskiy) and Vlad Yatsenko**. [Wikipedia via search result](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolut) ; [TechCrunch, 4 Nov 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/04/revoluts-valuation-jumps-53-to-115b-in-new-share-sale-as-it-prepares-to-launch-us-bank/)
- Founded at the Level39 fintech hub in Canary Wharf, London, initially as an app / prepaid multi-currency card letting UK and EEA users spend abroad at interbank FX rates. [CorpDigest history](https://corpdigest.com/company/revolut/company-history) ; [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolut)
- Storonsky was a former equity derivatives trader at Lehman Brothers and Credit Suisse; the founding idea is consistently reported as frustration with FX mark-ups / card fees when travelling. Yatsenko was a software engineer who had built financial / trading systems at banks including Credit Suisse, UBS and Deutsche Bank. [CorpDigest founders](https://corpdigest.com/company/revolut/founders) ; [Forge](https://forgeglobal.com/insights/revolut-upcoming-ipo-news/)
- Storonsky is reported to have initially self-funded the venture with about £300,000 of his own savings. [Wikipedia — Nik Storonsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nik_Storonsky)
- Legal founding nuance: Revolut Ltd was incorporated in December 2013 (Companies House entity 08804411, per Tracxn listing in search results), but the product / company is universally dated to its July 2015 launch. [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/revolut/__d_37XUuweMmGnnKKPXnToJLDE8Ra17xEkhMe-CpxtKI)
- Scale at latest disclosure: over **80 million retail customers** (Nov 2026, up from 68.3 million at 31 Dec 2025), operating in **40+ countries**, with banking operations across 30 EEA markets. 2025 group revenue **US$6.0bn (£4.5bn, +46%)**, pre-tax profit **US$2.3bn (£1.7bn, +57%, 38% margin)**, net profit US$1.7bn — fifth consecutive year of net profitability. [Revolut newsroom, 4 Nov 2026](http://revolut.com/en-FI/news/revolut_completes_fundraising_process_establishing_115_billion_valuation/) ; [Revolut 2025 results, 24 Mar 2026](https://www.revolut.com/en-AU/news/revolut_reports_record_profit_of_2_3bn_for_2025_as_revenue_surges_to_6bn/)

**Sources:** Revolut newsroom (above); TechCrunch (above); Wikipedia (above).

## 9. Main products

Retail and business, per Revolut's 2025 results and product reporting — 11 product lines each exceeded ~US$135m (£100m) revenue in 2025: [Revolut 2025 results](https://www.revolut.com/en-AU/news/revolut_reports_record_profit_of_2_3bn_for_2025_as_revenue_surges_to_6bn/)

- **Personal accounts & cards:** multi-currency current accounts, debit/prepaid cards, virtual/disposable cards, subscription plans (Standard through Ultra).
- **FX & international transfers:** hold/exchange 30+ currencies, spend in 160+ countries/regions at interbank rates (within plan limits); peer-to-peer transfers. FX revenue US$800m in 2025.
- **Payments:** card payments (US$1.3bn revenue in 2025), Revolut Pay (one-click / pay-by-bank checkout for merchants), merchant acquiring for business.
- **Savings:** Vaults / Instant Access Savings, interest on balances (interest income US$1.3bn in 2025).
- **Wealth & investing:** stock trading (US/EU, fractional), ETFs (zero-commission ETF plans in EEA/Switzerland, 2025), CFDs (29 countries), commodities, robo-advisory in select markets. Wealth revenue US$876m in 2025.
- **Crypto:** crypto trading (200+ tokens reported), staking, and Revolut X standalone crypto exchange (launched 2024).
- **Credit / lending:** personal loans, credit cards, overdrafts (market-dependent); lending portfolio grew 120% to US$2.9bn in 2025; mortgage refinancing launched in Lithuania (2025), mortgages in select markets.
- **Revolut Business:** multi-currency business accounts, corporate cards, invoicing, expenses, payroll, payment acceptance — launched Jun 2017; 767k business customers at end-2025 (800k+ by Nov 2026 reporting), 16% of group income in 2025.
- **Insurance & lifestyle:** travel/medical/device insurance in plans, RevPoints loyalty (17m users, 36 markets at end-2025), Stays travel booking, eSIM / Travel Mode.
- **Revolut Mobile:** mobile network service launched in UK and Poland (2025) — described by Revolut as a first for a large-scale financial institution.
- **Other:** Revolut <18 (junior) accounts, ATMs (first 50 own-branded ATMs launched in Spain, summer 2025), AI assistant AIR (rollout reported Apr 2026).
