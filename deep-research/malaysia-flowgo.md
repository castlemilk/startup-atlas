## 1. Overview & founding story

- FlowGo is "the AI accounting workforce that runs inside your Chat & Email" / "the chat-native financial automation layer for SMEs": businesses send invoices, purchase orders and receipts via WhatsApp, Telegram or email, and FlowGo's AI agents extract the data and post it directly into the company's accounting software. Sources: https://www.flowgoai.com/about ; https://www.startmate.com/portfolio-companies/flowgo
- **Founded:** October 2025. Source: https://www.flowgoai.com/about (At a Glance: "Founded, October 2025"). LinkedIn company data likewise gives founded year 2025. Source: https://linkedin.com/company/flowgoai
- **Founded by:** Anthony See and Jason Hor (see Section 2). Source: https://www.startmate.com/writing/a-500-order-starts-as-a-whatsapp-message-flowgo-handles-the-rest
- **Founding story (per Startmate):** The founders had worked on the "messy data" problem of SME ordering for years — Anthony previously scaled an F&B tech business across six countries, Jason was building the F&B POS systems they relied on. In 2024 they built a mobile app for suppliers so customers could order directly; it "didn't fly" because retailers/restaurant owners would not install separate apps, so they built FlowGo instead as an AI agent inside WhatsApp and email. The founders state that between them they have managed over US$15 billion in transaction volume and built workflows for more than 50,000 SMEs. Sources: https://www.startmate.com/writing/a-500-order-starts-as-a-whatsapp-message-flowgo-handles-the-rest ; https://www.flowgoai.com/blog/posts/startmate-s26-cohort
- **Legal entities:** FlowSync AI Sdn Bhd, Registration No. 202501045885 (1647293-D), Malaysia; and FlowGo Pty Ltd, ACN 691 432 292, Australia. Source: https://www.flowgoai.com/about

## 9. Main products

A single platform of named AI agents, hired individually, that take documents from WhatsApp, Telegram and email and post entries to accounting software (AutoCount, SQL Account, QNE Optimum, Xero, QuickBooks, SAP Business One, Biztory; MYOB and Oracle NetSuite listed as coming soon). Sources: https://www.flowgoai.com/about ; https://flowgoai.com/global/

Live agents (per About page):

- **Lizzie** — AP automation: invoice/receipt capture & posting

- **Sam** — Delivery & logistics: shipment tracking & proof of delivery
