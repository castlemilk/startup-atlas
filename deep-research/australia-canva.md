## 1. Overview & founding story

- Canva is an Australian online design and visual communication platform, launched in 2013, with a mission "to empower everyone in the world to design anything and publish anywhere." Source: https://www.canva.com/about/
- Founded by Melanie Perkins, Cliff Obrecht and Cameron Adams. Wikipedia lists: Founded 1 January 2013; Founders Melanie Perkins, Cliff Obrecht, Cameron Adams; Headquarters Sydney, Australia. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canva
- Other profiles record set-up in 2012: "In 2012 the couple moved to Sydney, recruited former Google designer Cameron Adams as a third co-founder and set up Canva, which opened to the public in August 2013." Source: https://softwarelore.com/companies/canva
- PitchBook profile lists Year Founded 2012. Source: https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/56364-67
- Origin: Perkins was teaching design software to university students in Perth and found programs like Photoshop/InDesign hard to learn. She and Obrecht first built Fusion Books in 2007, an online school-yearbook design tool, to prove simple drag-and-drop design could work. Sources: https://www.revenuememo.com/p/canva-funding ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melanie_Perkins
- Perkins and Obrecht were rejected by more than 100 investors. Through Google Maps co-creator Lars Rasmussen they were introduced to Cameron Adams, who joined in 2012 as third co-founder. Blackbird Ventures became the first major institutional investor, alongside a matching Australian government grant. The founders moved from Perth to Sydney to recruit technical talent, and Canva launched publicly in 2013. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melanie_Perkins
- Canva's own seed announcement (20 March 2013) confirms the Perth-to-Sydney story in contemporary form: "It's the second startup for Melanie Perkins and Cliff Obrecht, who previously launched online school yearbook design product Fusion Books in 2007... Cameron Adams, former lead designer at Google, has joined Canva as co-founder and Head of Product." Source: https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/aussie-startup-canva-announces-3-million-seed-round-world-class-investors/
- Scale at end-2025 (company-reported): 260 million people using Canva every month in the 2025 wrap (265 million monthly active users in later reporting of end-2025 ARR), $3.5 billion in revenue in 2025, and $4 billion ARR by end-2025. Sources: https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/canva-2025-wrap/ ; https://getlatka.com/companies/canva

## 9. Main products

- **Canva (core editor)** — browser/app design editor for presentations, social posts, posters, documents, video, websites and print, with template and stock library. Source: https://www.canva.com/about/
- **Visual Suite / Visual Suite 2.0** (2.0 launched 10 Apr 2025 at Canva Create) — single workspace/file combining Docs, Presentations, Whiteboards, Sheets, Websites, Video, Social and Print. Sources: https://companyview.io/software/canva ; https://Ceo.wiki/wiki/Canva
  - Canva Docs — visual documents
  - Canva Sheets — visual-first spreadsheets, with Magic Charts / Magic Formulas / Magic Insights
  - Canva Whiteboards — infinite collaborative canvas
  - Canva Websites, Canva Video, Canva Presentations, Print
- **Magic Studio** — AI suite launched Oct 2023: Magic Write (copy), Magic Design (design from prompt), Magic Media (image/video), Magic Edit, Magic Eraser, Background Remover, Magic Grab, Magic Resize / Magic Switch, Magic Translate, Dream Lab (Leonardo-powered image generation). Sources: https://companyview.io/software/canva ; https://www.equationseries.com/books/ai/companies/canva/
- **Canva AI / Canva AI 2.0** — conversational, agentic assistant. AI 2.0 launched in research preview 16 Apr 2026 at Canva Create in Los Angeles, powered by the Canva Design Model, with conversational design, agentic orchestration, object-based intelligence and "living memory", connectors, scheduling and Canva Code 2.0. Sources: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260416696203/en/Canva-Unveils-Canva-AI-2.0-Reimagining-How-The-World-Designs-and-Works ; https://siliconangle.com/2026/04/16/canva-unveils-canva-ai-2-0-recasting-platform-agentic-system-work/
- **Canva Code** — generates interactive widgets/apps (calculators, quizzes, mini-games) from a text prompt inside a design (launched Apr 2025). Source: https://companyview.io/software/canva
- **Affinity by Canva** — professional vector / pixel / page-layout suite (ex-Serif: Designer, Photo, Publisher), relaunched 30 Oct 2025 as a single unified app, free for core tools (Canva AI features require a paid Canva plan). Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canva ; https://www.marketing-interactive.com/canva-affinity-goes-pro-for-free-offering-creatives-a-new-alternative
- **Leonardo.ai** — AI image generation (Phoenix / Lucid models), acquired 2024, powers Dream Lab. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canva
- **Flourish** — data visualisation, acquired 2022. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canva
- **Canva Grow, Canva Enterprise / Business / Pro / Teams, Canva Education, Canva for Nonprofits** — tiers and segments. Creative Operating System launch (Oct 2025) introduced upgraded Video, Canva Grow (marketing engine) and a Canva Business tier. Sources: https://www.cmswire.com/digital-experience/canva-ai-20-adds-agentic-design-tools/ ; https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/one-year-canva-enterprise/
- **Canva ProSuite** (launched 15 Sep 2026 in reporting) — bundles Affinity, Cavalry, Flourish and Leonardo into one connected pro set. Source: https://theonly.bemosu.com/articles/canva-prosuite-affinity-3-3-blend-tool-indd-import/
