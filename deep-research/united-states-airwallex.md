## 1. Overview & founding story

Airwallex is a global payments and financial platform for businesses — described by the company as "a leading global payments and financial platform for modern businesses" and, in its June 2026 release, as "an AI native financial operating system for modern businesses." [1][2]

- **Founded:** 2015, in Melbourne, Australia. [1][3]
- **Founding story:** Software engineer Jack Zhang and architect Max Li had invested in a Melbourne coffee shop, Tukk & Co in Docklands, and found cross-border payments for imports costly and time-consuming for a small business. Zhang had previously designed digital FX trading platforms for National Australia Bank (NAB) and ANZ. Zhang and Li partnered with Lucy Liu and Xijing Dai — fellow University of Melbourne alumni — and Ki-Lok Wong. The founders invested a combined ~US$1 million of their own money. [3]
- The University of Melbourne summarises it as: "Founded in 2015 by four University of Melbourne alumni, Airwallex began with a clear ambition to build financial infrastructure better suited to a global, digital economy." [4]
- **Scale signals (company-reported):** In March 2026 Airwallex reached US$1.3 billion in annualised revenue, up 74% year-over-year, and US$287 billion in annualised transaction volume, up more than 120% year-over-year; it serves "more than 676,000 businesses worldwide, either directly or through our platform customers" and holds 85+ licences. [1]

## 3. Headquarters & other offices

**This is the key correction to the Atlas record — and the company's own wording supports the Atlas fact, with nuance:**

- **Current position (company's own words, June 2026):** "The company is co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore with over 2,300 employees across 27 offices." [1]
- **How it got there:** Airwallex announced in December 2025 that it had "established a second global headquarters in San Francisco" — i.e. San Francisco was added as a *second* / dual global HQ alongside Singapore. Its December 2025 boilerplate reads: "The company is co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore with over 2,000 employees across 26 offices." [5]
- **Singapore came first as global HQ:** Singapore Economic Development Board (EDB), republishing an Airwallex release dated 11 Nov 2026: "Since establishing its global headquarters in Singapore in 2023, Airwallex has significantly scaled its presence in the country" and Singapore is "one of its two global co-headquarters, alongside San Francisco." The Singapore co-HQ is at Guoco Tower, Tanjong Pagar, expanding to over 80,000 sq ft across four floors. [6]
- **Earlier history (press/reference, not the company's current boilerplate):** Founded in Melbourne (2015); headquarters moved Melbourne → Hong Kong in 2018, then to Singapore in 2023. [3][7] In 2025 the company said it had "moved into a permanent U.S. headquarters in San Francisco" (2024) — wording that preceded the formal December 2025 "second global headquarters" designation. [2]
- **LinkedIn company page** lists Headquarters as San Francisco, California, primary address 188 Spear St, San Francisco, CA 94105, US, and states "co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore." Other locations listed include Melbourne (Level 7, 15 William St), Hong Kong (Oxford House, King's Road), Shanghai, London (1 Long Lane) and Singapore (Guoco Tower, 1 Wallich Street). [8]
- **Legal domicile:** Press/reporting describes the group parent, Airwallex (Cayman) Limited, as incorporated in the Cayman Islands. This is a domicile fact, not an operating HQ. [7]

**Bottom line for Atlas:** "San Francisco, United States and Singapore" as a dual HQ is **correct per the company's own current boilerplate** — but it is a *co-headquarters* arrangement dating from December 2025; Singapore was the sole global HQ from 2023 until then, and Melbourne is the founding city (still a major office, Level 7, 15 William St). [1][5][6][8]

## 9. Main products

From Airwallex's own product descriptions:

- **Business Accounts / Global Accounts** — multi-currency accounts, local account details, FX conversion, international transfers, and Yield on balances. [18][2]
- **Payments (Payments Acceptance)** — online and in-store payment acceptance, cards and 160+ local payment methods, multi-currency checkout. [2]
- **Spend** — multi-currency corporate and employee cards, expense management, bill payments / Bill Pay, AI-assisted spend agents. [2][5]
- **Billing** — invoicing, subscription management and usage-based billing (expanded via the OpenPay acquisition, §12). [18]
- **Platform APIs & Embedded Finance** — programmable accounts, payments, payouts, FX and card issuing for platforms to embed and monetise financial services. [2][18]
- **Treasury / Yield** — earn returns on idle balances via money-market funds (J.P. Morgan Asset Management funds in the Qantas/Australia description). [18][19]
- **New in June 2026:** **T:0** — an AI-native finance platform to automate bookkeeping, forecasting, taxes, compliance and reporting (private beta at announcement; launched 1 Sep 2026 per newsroom index); **Airi** — an agentic consumer wallet / wallet infrastructure for agentic commerce, incorporating one-click checkout. [1][20]
