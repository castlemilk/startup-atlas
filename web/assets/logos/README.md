These brand marks are self-hosted for identification; their inclusion does not imply a commercial relationship or eligibility for an offer. Unreviewed or ambiguous names deliberately fall back to initials. The names-to-logos mapping is maintained in `logos.js`.

- The following SVGs are from [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/tree/master/packages/static-svg/icons): `openai`, `aws-color`, `microsoft-color`, `xai`, `together`, `fireworks`, `baseten`, `deepinfra`, `nebius`, `replit`, `devin`, `cerebras`, `alibabacloud`, `github`, `googlecloud`, `anthropic`, `vercel`, `google-color` and `nvidia-color`. Lobe Icons is distributed under the MIT license; see `lobe-icons-MIT.txt`.
- `modal`, `producthunt`, `ycombinator`, `cloudflare`, `neon`, `mongodb`, `elevenlabs`, `deepgram`, `posthog`, `digitalocean`, `vultr`, `stripe`, `atlassian`, `notion`, `intercom` and `hubspot` are from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons). Brand names and marks remain the property of their respective owners. Before adding a new logo, establish the identity and record its source and applicable terms.

## Startup logo sources (added 27 November 2026)

The startup marks added in this batch (`canva`, `fusion-books`, `airwallex`, `bugcrowd`, `morse-micro`, `tendor`, `lockii`, `draper`, `gilmour-space`, `uluu`, `go1`, `cauldron`, `eucalyptus`, `loam-bio`, `pi-exchange`, `wayve`, `diraq`, `emesent`, `fluency`, `andromeda-robotics`, `floodmapp`) were taken from each company's own website head assets (JSON-LD `logo`, header logo, apple-touch-icon or icon) and visually verified against the company identity; each record's `logo_source` in `data/startup-research.json` is the company's homepage. Exceptions: Canva's homepage is bot-walled from the build environment, so its JSON-LD logo was read from an archived capture of canva.com and downloaded live from static.canva.com; Fusion Books' live domain now redirects to Canva, so its mark comes from an archived capture of its own site (November 2022); Go1's and Loam Bio's header logos are inline SVGs extracted from their homepages with the site's own logo colour applied; the Wise, Revolut and UiPath records point at the pre-existing Simple Icons files above. PsiQuantum is deliberately not included: its record documents that Atlas does not republish the logo while usage permission remains unresolved.
