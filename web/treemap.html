How to read the treemap

Recorded valuation. The latest dated, positive numeric valuation for each company in the selected currency. Each observation retains its announcement date and original source.

Funding announcement. The latest dated, positive numeric event amount in the selected currency. Events can include secondary transfers and other financing. The chart measures individual announcements.

Company count. In the treemap, companies have equal area. Cards use fixed layout sizes. Country and city refer to recorded headquarters. Founding decades use recorded founding years.

Coverage. Exact values require an announced date, a numeric figure and a stated currency. Currencies stay separate. Records with zero, undisclosed or unavailable figures are accessible below the results. Public-company market capitalisation is unavailable.
