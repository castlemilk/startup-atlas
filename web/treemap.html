READING THE LANDSCAPE

Recorded, not real-time.

Valuation: the latest dated, positive numeric valuation recorded for each company in the selected currency. These historical announcements are from different dates, not current market values or a ranking of every startup.

Funding announcement: the latest dated, positive numeric event amount in the selected currency. It is not total funding raised. Events may include secondary transfers or other financing; read each source note.

Market cap: public-company equity market capitalisation is not recorded in this dataset. Private valuations and funding announcements are not substitutes.

Coverage: newer announcements with unrecorded dates, amounts or currencies may be omitted. Currencies are never converted or combined. Company count gives every company equal area, including those with no financial data. Founding decades use recorded founding years only; missing years stay “Not recorded”.
