How to read the treemap

Company value. Listed companies use their latest sourced market cap. Privately held companies use their latest sourced company valuation. Each tile identifies its figure and date. The map uses one currency at a time and can include observations from different dates.

Public market cap. Dated values for publicly listed companies in the selected currency. An estimate uses the closing price and the disclosed shares with economic rights. Each figure shows its sources and dates.

Recorded valuation. The latest dated, positive numeric valuation for each company in the selected currency. Each observation retains its announcement date and original source.

Funding announcement. The latest dated, positive numeric event amount in the selected currency. Events can include secondary transfers and other financing. The chart measures individual announcements.

Company count. In the treemap, companies have equal area. Cards use fixed layout sizes.

Grouping. Technology type follows the core product described in each company's reviewed source. Each company contributes to one group. Country and city refer to recorded headquarters. Founding decades use recorded founding years.

Coverage. Figures require a date, an amount, a stated currency and a source. Currencies stay separate. Records with zero, undisclosed or unavailable figures are accessible below the results.
